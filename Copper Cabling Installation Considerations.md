Building rules and fire codes say which cable you must use in different areas.

### Plenum Cable

- **What is a plenum?** The empty space used for heating and air conditioning (HVAC)—usually above a drop ceiling or under a raised floor. Cables are often run here too.
    
- **Fire Hazard:** Plenum spaces have lots of airflow and no walls to stop fire. If used for heating, they can also get hot.
    
- **Safety Rules:** Plenum cable must be fire-retardant. It must put itself out (self-extinguish), stop fire from spreading, and not produce heavy smoke.
    
- **Materials:**
    
    - **Regular cable:** Uses standard PVC plastic.
        
    - **Plenum cable:** Uses treated PVC or FEP plastic. This makes the cable stiffer, but it does **not** slow down internet speed.
        
- **Labels:**
    
    - **CMP:** Safe for plenum spaces.
        
    - **CM / CMG:** Standard cable for basic indoor walls (homes or single-story buildings).
        

### Outdoor & Direct Burial Cable

Outside cables face sun, rain, and extreme temperatures. Standard PVC cables will break down outdoors.

- **Aerial Cable:** Hung between poles. Needs protection from sunlight (UV rays), rain, and heat/cold.
    
- **Conduit Runs:** Cable put inside protective pipes. Still needs protection against moisture and temperature changes.
    
- **Direct Burial Cable:** Cable laid straight into dirt, concrete, or cement with no pipe around it.
    
- **Outdoor Protections:** Special outer coatings against sun and scraping, waterproof gel inside, and metal armor to stop rodents from chewing through.
