Inkjet printers create images by spraying tiny ink droplets directly onto the paper, whereas laser printers use a drum to transfer toner to the paper through an electrostatic process.

Laser printers use a laser to create an electrostatic image on a drum, while inkjet printers spray ink droplets onto paper.

Inkjet printers do not use heat and pressure; they spray ink droplets. Laser printers use heat and pressure in the fusing stage, and piezoelectric elements are used in some inkjet printers, not laser printers.

The primary charge roller is part of the laser printing process, not inkjet. Inkjet printers use a carriage system to move the printhead, not laser printers.

A tractor feed. Continuous computer paper features perforated holes along both edges. A tractor feed uses rotating sprockets with pins that fit into these holes to pull the connected sheets steadily through the printer without slipping. This setup is standard on impact and dot matrix printers used for data logging.
