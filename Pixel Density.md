Higher density results in sharper images.
	- Refresh Rates: Higher rates (measured in Hz) provide smoother motion, which is important for gaming and video playback.
	- Screen Resolution: Defines clarity and detail of the display. Common resolutions include:
	    - Full HD (1920x1080)
	    - Quad HD (2560x1440)
	    - 4K (3840x2160)
	    - Newer Resolutions: 5K (5120x2880) and 8K (7680x4320) are emerging, offering even higher clarity and detail.
	- Color Gamut: Refers to the range of colors a display can produce based on the RGB color model. A wider color gamut allows for more vibrant and accurate color reproduction, enhancing the visual experience for activities such as photo editing, video production, and gaming. Common color gamuts include sRGB, Adobe RGB, and DCI-P3, with DCI-P3 offering a broader range of colors compared to sRGB. Additionally, color depth plays a crucial role, with 24-bit color (often referred to as true color) supporting approximately 16.7 million colors, while 32-bit color includes an alpha channel for transparency, providing even more detailed color representation and smoother gradients.
