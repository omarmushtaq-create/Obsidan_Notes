## Coaxial Cable (Coax)

Coaxial cable is a type of copper cable that carries electrical signals. Unlike twisted pair cable (which cancels out interference by balancing wires), coax places two conductors along the **same central axis**.

### Construction Layers

- **Core Conductor:** The center wire that carries the signal.
    
- **Insulation (Dielectric):** A plastic layer that covers the center core.
    
- **Shielding / Ground:** A second outer wire mesh layer that blocks electrical interference (EMI) and acts as a ground.
    
- **Outer Jacket:** The protective exterior coating.
    

### Common Uses

- CCTV security cameras
    
- Cable Access TV (CATV) patch cables
    
- Broadband internet connections
    

### Connector Types

- **F-Type Connector:** Uses a screw-down design; commonly used for CATV installations.
    
- **BNC (Bayonet Neill-Concelman) Connector:** Uses a push-and-twist bayonet locking mechanism; commonly used for video, radio, and television equipment.
