**Unmanaged**

- Unmanaged switched perform can perform their fuctions without any setup. You can just power it and then connect some hosts and your done
- Common unmanaged switches will have four or eight ports becuase they are mostly used in small networks.
- There is an unmanaged four-port switch embedded in most of the SOHO router/modems supplied by Internet Service Providers (ISPs) to connect to their networks.
