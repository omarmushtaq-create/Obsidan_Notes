(**Small office/home office LAN**)



- A small network using a one centeral server along with client devices,.
- Offten uses a single setworking applince to provide LAN and Internet (This is often referred to as a "SOHO router," "Internet router," or "broadband router.")
- SOHO networks ae often designed to only support a small amount of people
