- Switches are used to connect multiple devices togther with Ethernet
- When a device is connected to the switch, the switch adds its MAC adress to a table inside of it to keep track of what ports its connected to.
- When a frame (A frame is a packet of data with a header payload and trailer), the switch decodes the header and sends the frame to the correct place/![A switch operation process.](https://cdn.testout.com/a-plus-220-120x-en-us/materials/resources/text/s_networking_hardware/508-1636621766234-net_fig03_03.png)

This means that each switch port is considered a separate collision domain, and the negative effects of collisions are eliminated. Each computer has a full duplex connection to the network and can send and receive simultaneously at the full speed supported by the network cabling and NIC.
