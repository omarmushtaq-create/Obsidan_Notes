

## Overview
- Operates in **2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz** bands (all three)
- Uses **320 MHz wide channels** in the 6 GHz range
- Max data transfer speed: up to **46 Gbps**

---

## Multi-Link Operation (MLO)
- Allows a device to connect and send data over **multiple bands/channels at the same time**
  - e.g., 2.4 GHz + 5 GHz + 6 GHz simultaneously
- Benefits:
  - **Reduces latency**
  - **Improves throughput**

---

## Multi-Resource Units (MRUs)
- Each 6 GHz channel is broken into smaller sub-channels called **MRUs**
- MRUs can be **different sizes** depending on network needs
- AP **dynamically allocates** MRUs to devices based on their bandwidth needs
  - High bandwidth need -> **more MRUs**
  - Low bandwidth need -> **fewer MRUs**
- Improves efficiency and flexibility of channel usage

---

## Quick Summary
- Wi-Fi 7 = fastest standard yet (up to 46 Gbps)
- Key new features: **MLO** (multi-band connections) + **MRU** (smart sub-channel allocation)
- Works across all 3 bands: 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, 6 GHz
